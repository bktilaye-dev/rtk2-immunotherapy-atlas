Not medical advice. This atlas is a research-organization aid for discussion with a treating neuro-oncologist. RTK II is a DNA-methylation subclass identified after surgery, not a treatment indication most trials enroll by — nearly every entry below enrolls on IDH-wildtype glioblastoma histology broadly, not RTK II status specifically. Evidence that a subtype-stratified DNA-methylation signature correlates with tumor T-cell infiltration and survival exists (Neuro-Oncology, 2021) but subtype-specific immunotherapy outcome data remains sparse — treat mechanism fit, not subtype-matching, as the reason each entry is listed.

How the evidence grade works

Grading basis: stage reflects the most advanced publicly reported clinical stage for that specific approach — not a claim of proven efficacy at that stage. A Phase 1 entry with a strong early signal (e.g. the ANKTIVA + NK combination below) is noted in its description; grade alone does not encode response rate.

Scope: "recruiting" reflects registry status at time of research (Sep 2026) and can change — always re-check ClinicalTrials.gov, the EU CTR, or the named registry before contacting a site. Locations list trial sponsor/site geography where known; several device- and cell-therapy trials run at a small number of specialized centers only.
